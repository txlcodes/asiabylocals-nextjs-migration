5 Must-See Places in Agra

Beyond the Taj Mahal — what every first-timer should visit in India's most iconic city.

By AsiaByLocals
01

Taj Mahal

No Agra visit is complete without the Taj. Built in 1632 by Emperor Shah Jahan as a monument of love, it took 22 years and 20,000 workers to complete.

Tip: Go at sunrise to avoid crowds and see the marble change colour in the golden light.
02

Agra Fort

This massive red sandstone fortress was the seat of the Mughal Empire for generations. Shah Jahan was imprisoned here by his own son — with a view of the Taj Mahal from his cell.

Tip: Hire a local guide — the stories behind each palace within the fort make it 10x better.
03

Fatehpur Sikri

A ghost city 40 km from Agra. Emperor Akbar built this entire capital in 1571, then abandoned it after just 14 years due to water shortage. The architecture is stunning and untouched.

Tip: Visit in the afternoon when tour buses leave. You'll have the Panch Mahal almost to yourself.
04

Mehtab Bagh

The best-kept secret in Agra. This garden across the Yamuna River gives you a free, unobstructed sunset view of the Taj Mahal — the same view Shah Jahan designed it to be seen from.

Tip: Arrive 1 hour before sunset. Entry is just 50 rupees (~$0.60).
05

Itimad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj)

Built 20 years before the Taj Mahal, this "Baby Taj" is considered its draft version. Smaller, quieter, and with even more detailed marble inlay work — and almost no tourists.

Tip: The marble lattice screens here are more intricate than the Taj. Bring a zoom lens.
